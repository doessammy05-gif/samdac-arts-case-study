# Academic Framework: Samdac Arts

## Theoretical Basis
This project utilizes the **Technology Acceptance Model (TAM)**. By reducing the 'Perceived Cost' (via comrade pricing) and increasing 'Perceived Usefulness' (via AI automation), the framework accelerates the adoption of professional digital tools among student entrepreneurs.

## Methodology
1. **Needs Assessment:** Identification of common pain points in student business social media pages.
2. **Architecture Design:** Development of Next.js templates optimized for mobile-first campus users.
3. **Implementation:** Deployment of landing pages with integrated lead-capture forms.
4. **Evaluation:** Comparison of client acquisition rates before and after deployment.
